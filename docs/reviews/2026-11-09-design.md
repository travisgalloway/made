### DSN-48156e88

- Location: docs/architecture/invariants.md:27
- Pass: D1 ()
- Severity: medium
- Verdict: CONFIRMED
- Evidence: invariants.md:27 states the arbiter 'locks mode on the first movement and holds it until release'. PointerRouter.swift:60-72 answers a .mouse move with 'let mode: GestureMode = state.draggedPastThreshold ? .navigate : .manipulate' and 'return GestureDecision(mode: mode, locked: state.draggedPastThreshold)', so the first 6 px of every orbit, pan or zoom drag report .manipulate with locked false. CADMetalViewRepresentable.swift:1561 runs '} else if decision.mode == .manipulate { resolveSnap(at: loc, in: view) }' on those moves. The mode is not locked on first movement and can flip from manipulate to navigate within one gesture.
- Disposition: parked (docs/parked-findings.md)

### DSN-f5402406

- Location: macos/Sources/MadeCore/Agent/MCPServer.swift:116

- Verdict: PLAUSIBLE
